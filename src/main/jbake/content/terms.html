The agreement

Asymm Systems apps on the Atlassian Marketplace are provided under the Atlassian Marketplace standard end user agreement: the Bonterms Standard End User Agreement, Version 1.0. Asymm Systems is the Provider under that agreement.

We add no provider-specific terms other than our support policy, below. Fees and payment are as stated in your order on the Atlassian Marketplace.
