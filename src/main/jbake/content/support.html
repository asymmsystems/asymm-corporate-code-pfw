Asymm Systems

Support

How to reach us about an Asymm Systems app for Jira Cloud, such as CascadePlan, and how fast we answer.

Contact

  • Support: support@asymm.systems
  • Security reports: security@asymm.systems
  • Privacy and data requests: privacy@asymm.systems

Please tell us the app, your Atlassian site address, what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Response times

  • General questions and defects: a first answer within 24 business hours, Monday to Friday.
  • A defect that blocks your production work: within 4 hours.
  • Security reports: acknowledged within 72 hours.
  • Privacy questions: within 7 days; data requests within 30 days.

Documentation

The user guides describe each app; the FAQ answers common questions.
