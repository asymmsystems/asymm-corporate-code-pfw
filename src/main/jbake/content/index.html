ODA Compliance Verification for Telecom

The developer ecosystem that verifies TMF Open Digital Architecture conformance during development — not during integration testing.

Prototype complete. Launching 2026. Learn more

We built Asymm JDK because telecom workloads deserve a JDK that understands their environment. We built Dial because ODA compliance shouldn't be discovered during integration testing. Both share a conviction: industry standards should be verified automatically, during development, not manually, after deployment.

Our founder is a contributor to Spring Cloud Netflix, Spring Boot, and Debezium. We believe in giving back to the JVM ecosystem that powers our products.
