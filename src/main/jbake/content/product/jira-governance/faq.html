FAQ & Licensing

Frequently Asked Questions & Licensing

Answers to common questions regarding Atlassian Marketplace licensing, billing mechanics, data residency, Cloud Fortified status, and technical support.

1. Licensing & Billing Mechanics

How does the 1 - 10 user free tier work?

Every application in the Asymm Systems Jira Governance Suite is completely free for Jira instances with up to 10 users. The free tier is not a crippled trial: it includes 100% of features, unlimited seals, all workflow validators, and the full audit history, with no time limit. Small teams, startups, and evaluation pods can run the suite in production indefinitely without entering a credit card.

How is billing calculated for larger instances?

Billing is managed directly by Atlassian via your standard Jira Cloud invoice. Atlassian Marketplace licenses apps based on the user tier of your Jira Cloud instance (e.g. a 50-user Jira instance requires a 50-user app tier). Pricing scales down with volume:

  • 1 - 10 Users: Free forever.
  • 11 - 100 Users: $1.50 per user per month.
  • 101 - 250 Users: $1.20 per user per month.
  • 251 - 1,000 Users: $0.90 per user per month.

Do you offer annual payment discounts?

Yes. Choosing annual billing via Atlassian Marketplace grants 2 months free per year (pay for 10 months, receive 12 months of coverage).

Can I purchase apps individually, or do I need the whole suite?

Each app (LedgerSeal, DefinitionGuard, FourRealities, CascadePlan) is published as an independent Atlassian Marketplace app. You can install only the specific capabilities your compliance framework demands (e.g. LedgerSeal for change control seals), or deploy all four to establish an end-to-end zero-trust governance layer.

2. Architecture & Data Residency

Where is customer data stored?

All application data (cryptographic hashes, seal records, DoD checklist items, and plans) is stored only in Atlassian Forge storage for your installation, and some of it as Jira issue properties, remote links and comments on your own site. Data residency follows Atlassian's rules for Forge apps.

Does any data leave our Atlassian perimeter?

No. Every app in the suite declares zero outbound egress endpoints in its Atlassian Forge manifest. We operate no external SaaS backends, databases, or API proxies. All hashing, signing, and verification logic runs on Atlassian Forge. The apps call only the Jira and Confluence APIs of your own Atlassian site.

Are Asymm Systems apps Cloud Fortified?

No. The apps are not Cloud Fortified. They are built on Atlassian Forge with no external egress, and their data stays in Atlassian Forge storage.

What personal data do the apps store?

CascadePlan stores Atlassian account ids only: of who froze a baseline, of who requested or reviewed a scope change, and of each person who works on a task through a Jira sub-task. It reports them each week to Atlassian's personal data reporting service and removes the id of a closed account.

The other three apps store Atlassian account ids to record who did each action: LedgerSeal of who sealed, signed or revoked a seal (with the signer's display name), DefinitionGuard of who ticked, waived or configured a Definition of Done item, and FourRealities of who requested or approved a gate exception. The privacy policy lists what each app stores.

3. Support & Incident SLAs

How do I obtain technical support?

Commercial license holders have access to priority engineering support:

  • Support Email: support@asymm.systems
  • Response SLA: Within 24 business hours (Monday through Friday) for general inquiries; within 4 hours for production blocking transition defects.
  • Security Issues: Report vulnerabilities directly to security@asymm.systems (acknowledged within 72 hours).
