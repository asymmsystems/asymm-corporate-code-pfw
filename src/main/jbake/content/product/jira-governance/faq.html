FAQ & Licensing

Frequently Asked Questions & Licensing

Answers to common questions regarding Atlassian Marketplace licensing, billing mechanics, data residency, Cloud Fortified status, and technical support SLAs.

1. Licensing & Billing Mechanics

How does the 1 - 10 user free tier work?

Every application in the Asymm Systems Jira Governance Suite is completely free for Jira instances with up to 10 users. The free tier is not a crippled trial: it includes 100% of features, unlimited seals, all workflow validators, and full audit exports forever. Small teams, startups, and evaluation pods can run the suite in production indefinitely without entering a credit card.

How is billing calculated for larger instances?

Billing is managed directly by Atlassian via your standard Jira Cloud invoice. Atlassian Marketplace licenses apps based on the user tier of your Jira Cloud instance (e.g. a 50-user Jira instance requires a 50-user app tier). Pricing scales down with volume:

  • 1 - 10 Users: Free forever.
  • 11 - 100 Users: $1.50 per user per month.
  • 101 - 250 Users: $1.20 per user per month.
  • 251 - 1,000 Users: $0.90 per user per month.

Do you offer annual payment discounts?

Yes. Choosing annual billing via Atlassian Marketplace grants 2 months free per year (pay for 10 months, receive 12 months of coverage).

Can I purchase apps individually, or do I need the whole suite?

Each app (LedgerSeal, DefinitionGuard, FourRealities, CascadePlan) is published as an independent Atlassian Marketplace app. You can install only the specific capabilities your compliance framework demands (e.g. LedgerSeal for change control seals), or deploy all four to establish an end-to-end zero-trust governance layer.

2. Architecture & Data Residency

Where is customer data stored?

All application data (cryptographic hashes, seal metadata, DoD checklist items, and baseline snapshots) is persisted exclusively in Atlassian Forge Key-Value Storage (KVS) inside your own Atlassian tenant. Data residency aligns with your Jira Cloud instance region.

Does any data leave our Atlassian perimeter?

No. Every app in the suite declares zero outbound egress endpoints in its Atlassian Forge manifest. We operate no external SaaS backends, databases, or API proxies. All hashing, signing, and verification logic runs inside Atlassian micro-runtimes.

Are Asymm Systems apps Cloud Fortified?

Not yet. The Atlassian Cloud Fortified program requires 12 months of active marketplace tenure and specific minimum installation volume. Our apps are built to exceed Cloud Fortified technical standards from day one (100% Forge native, zero egress, tenant data residency). Formal Cloud Fortified application will occur once marketplace tenure criteria are satisfied.

3. Support & Incident SLAs

How do I obtain technical support?

Commercial license holders have access to priority engineering support:

  • Support Email: support@asymm.systems

  • Security Issues: Report vulnerabilities directly to security@asymm.systems (acknowledged within 72 hours).
