Everything you need to evaluate, configure, operate, and audit the Asymm Systems Jira Governance Suite. Organized by application and role: jump directly to the chapter you need.

5-Minute Quickstart Evaluation

Follow these five steps to verify zero-trust governance in your Jira Cloud instance in under five minutes:

Step 1: Install App

Install any suite app from the Atlassian Marketplace into your Jira Cloud instance. Teams with 1-10 users enjoy full features free of charge forever.

Step 2: Check Permissions

Navigate to Apps > Asymm Governance Admin. Ensure your role is granted Administer or Approver permission per the Uniform Permission Standard.

Step 3: Seal or Review

Open a test Jira issue. Seal it with LedgerSeal, complete a DefinitionGuard checklist, or review reality alignment in FourRealities.

Step 4: Test Workflow Gate

Configure a workflow validator on your project transition (e.g. into Done or In Review). Attempt to transition an unsealed or ungated issue.

Step 5: Export Evidence

Download the standalone evidence JSON certificate and verify it offline with the zero-dependency Node.js CLI verifier tool.
