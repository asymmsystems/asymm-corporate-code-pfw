CascadePlan Guide

CascadePlan User & Administrator Guide

How to structure project delivery with interactive Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) trees, freeze sprint baselines, detect and quantify mid-sprint scope creep, balance team workloads with resource heatmaps, and govern commitment changes through formal shift approvals.

1. The Silent Scope Creep Problem

In high-velocity software engineering, sprint commitments erode quietly. During sprint planning, a team commits to a discrete set of deliverables. Over the next two weeks, additional tasks are inserted, issue estimates expand, and unplanned bugs are attached without leadership visibility. At the end of the sprint, the team misses their target, yet standard Jira reports cannot distinguish between failed execution and unapproved scope expansion.

CascadePlan introduces formal sprint baseline governance. At sprint planning cutoff, the project manager freezes a cryptographic baseline snapshot. CascadePlan continuously computes commitment deltas (+%), highlights mid-flight scope creep, and requires authorized shift approvals before baselines can be modified.

2. Visual Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) Planner

CascadePlan replaces flat Jira backlogs with an interactive, hierarchical Work Breakdown Structure:

  • Multi-Level Hierarchy: Organize Epics, Stories, Sub-tasks, and Spikes in a clean, expandable tree structure.
  • Inline Editing: Update estimates, assignees, and priorities directly inside the planner without opening individual Jira issue pages.
  • Dependency Mapping: Visualize blockers and task sequencing directly on the board.

3. Resource Allocation Utilization Heatmap

Prevent team burnout and bottlenecking with real-time capacity visualization:

  • Assignee x Time Grid: View committed hours and story points allocated to each engineer across upcoming weeks and sprints.
  • Visual Utilization Heatmap: Color-coded thresholds highlight balanced workload (green), near-capacity (amber), and over-allocated engineers (red).
  • Rebalancing: Drag tasks between assignees in the grid to balance velocity before the sprint begins.

Figure 2: Resource allocation utilization heatmap identifying capacity bottlenecks.

4. Freezing Sprint Baselines & Scope Creep Alerts

To lock in a sprint commitment:

  1. At the conclusion of sprint planning, click Freeze Baseline.
  2. CascadePlan captures an immutable snapshot of all committed issues, estimates, and assignees.
  3. As the sprint progresses, any newly inserted ticket or modified estimate triggers an automated commitment delta calculation.
  4. When scope expansion exceeds your configured threshold (e.g. +10%), CascadePlan raises a prominent Scope Creep Alert badge.

5. Formal Shift Approval Governance

When additional scope is mandatory and approved by management:

  1. A project lead clicks Request Shift Approval on the baseline view.
  2. The modal summarizes the variance (e.g. +8 Story Points, 2 New Issues Added).
  3. An authorized approver enters a formal change rationale (e.g. Executive priority: Expedited client compliance deliverable per Q4 steering committee).
  4. Upon confirmation, a new baseline revision is stamped, resetting the scope delta to 0% while preserving historical baseline revisions for audit review.
