Compliance & Security

Enterprise Compliance & Regulatory Mapping

Detailed control mappings for SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and FDA 21 CFR Part 11, alongside pre-filled InfoSec vendor assessment answers for enterprise security procurement.

Compliance Disclaimer & Boundaries

Asymm Systems apps provide technical enforcement mechanisms that support compliance with industry frameworks. However, software tools alone cannot confer certification. Your external qualified auditor decides whether your change management policies and implementation satisfy regulatory mandates. Customers remain responsible for validating standard operating procedures, training personnel, and configuring appropriate workflow gates within Jira Cloud.

1. SOC 2 Type II (Trust Services Criteria CC8.1)

AICPA TSC CC8.1 mandates that organizations authorize, design, develop or acquire, configure, test, approve, and implement changes to infrastructure, data, software, and procedures:

SOC 2 CC8.1 Control Focus Asymm Suite App Technical Enforcement Mechanism
Formal Change Authorization LedgerSeal Captures Ed25519 digital signatures and SHA-256 snapshots upon approval. Rejects unapproved release transitions via Jira workflow validators.
Post-Approval Integrity LedgerSeal Detects modifications to ticket descriptions, criteria, or attachments post-approval. Alerts approvers and blocks release until resealed.
Separation of Duties DefinitionGuard & LedgerSeal Enforces non-assignee peer reviews on DoD criteria. Prevents the author of post-approval changes from resealing their own ticket.
Verification & Testing Evidence DefinitionGuard Requires mandatory URL links to CI test reports, security scans, and code reviews before an issue can transition into Done.
Scope Creep & Baseline Control CascadePlan Freezes sprint baselines and flags scope variance (+%), ensuring unplanned work receives formal managerial authorization.

2. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (Control A.8.32 - Change Management)

ISO 27001 Control A.8.32 requires that changes to information processing facilities and information systems be subject to change management procedures:

ISO 27001 A.8.32 Requirement Asymm Suite Implementation
Identification & Record of Changes All ticket modifications, seal creations, reseals, DoD item completions, and baseline freezes record ISO-8601 timestamps and user account IDs.
Evaluation of Potential Impact FourRealities evaluates cross-reality drift (RAI) to prevent unreviewed architectural impact between Confluence specifications and Git code.
Formal Approval Requirements Workflow validators enforce mandatory approval seals and completed Definition of Done items prior to release state transitions.
Audit Trail Continuity LedgerSeal implements cryptographic hash chaining, where each reseal block references the SHA-256 digest of the preceding block.

3. FDA 21 CFR Part 11 (Electronic Records & Signatures)

For life sciences, pharmaceutical, and medical device organizations subject to FDA 21 CFR Part 11 regulations:

21 CFR Part 11 Section Regulatory Requirement LedgerSeal Technical Implementation
§ 11.10(e) Audit Trails Secure, computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails to independently record operator actions. LedgerSeal captures immutable, hash-chained records of seals, detected modifications, justifications, and reseals. Exportable to standalone JSON.
§ 11.50 Signature Manifestations Signatures must manifest: printed name of signer, date/time, and meaning (review, approval, responsibility). Every seal explicitly records and displays the approver Atlassian account ID, ISO-8601 timestamp, and selectable declaration meaning.
§ 11.70 Signature Linking Electronic signatures must be linked to their respective records to ensure signatures cannot be excised or transferred. Ed25519 digital signatures are computed directly over the canonical SHA-256 snapshot hash of ticket fields, rendering signatures non-transferable.

4. Vendor Security Assessment (CAIQ-Lite / SIG-Lite)

Pre-filled answers to standard enterprise InfoSec vendor questionnaires:

Questionnaire Domain Standard Question Asymm Systems Answer
Architecture & Hosting Where does application compute and storage reside? 100% within Atlassian Cloud. Compute runs on Atlassian Forge Lambda runtimes. Data is persisted in customer tenant Atlassian Forge KVS. Asymm operates no external servers.
