Eliya for your industry

Eliya is built for compliance-conscious production. The premise is the same in every regulated sector: some production-evidence and compliance requirements cannot be set from outside the runtime, so Eliya puts the policy point inside the JVM. What differs is the specific requirement each industry runs into first.

  • OpenJDK for telecom: carrier-grade incident evidence across BSS and OSS, kept inside the operator perimeter.
  • OpenJDK for banking and BFSI: a heap dump of a payment service is at-rest cardholder data (PCI DSS 3.5.1), a dump-writer problem inside the VM.
  • OpenJDK for healthcare: heap dumps and logs can hold PHI at rest; diagnostic capture stays local, never a SaaS.
  • OpenJDK for government: protected crash-path logs, attestable flag origin, reproducible signed builds, data sovereignty.

Each page is honest about what ships in 25.0.3 today versus what is on the roadmap. Compliance-aligned profile values are demand-gated and not shipping yet.
