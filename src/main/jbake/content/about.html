About Asymm Systems

The Company

Founded: 2026
Location: Sri Lanka
Focus: Compliance

Asymm Systems builds developer tools that automate compliance verification for standards-driven industries. Our name reflects a core belief in asymmetrical leverage - building tools that give engineers disproportionate control over systemic complexity.

Today, every standards-driven industry faces the same bottleneck - specifications exist as documents, but compliance is verified during integration testing or certification - late, expensive and avoidable. We solve this by shifting compliance left - turning industry specifications into development ecosystem constructs. This transforms compliance from a late, expensive, and avoidable risk into a build-time guarantee.

We started with telecom because that's where we have 15 years of domain expertise and where the pain is most acute. With over 186 organisations moving toward Open Digital Architecture, the demand for automated conformance is global. The approach is designed to extend to any industry with formal specifications.
