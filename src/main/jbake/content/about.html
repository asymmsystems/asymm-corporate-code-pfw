The Company

Asymm Systems builds developer tools that automate compliance verification for standards-driven industries.

The telecom industry is undergoing a massive architectural shift. 186 organisations have signed the TM Forum Open Digital Architecture manifesto. 18 CSPs are Running on ODA. Over 1,700 Open API deployments are certified. Two billion subscribers are served globally.

Yet the development tools haven't kept up. Engineers build ODA components with no automated way to verify conformance during development. Compliance issues are discovered during integration testing or certification — late, expensive, and avoidable.

We started with telecom because that's where we have 15 years of domain expertise and where the pain is most acute. The approach is designed to extend to any industry with formal specifications.
