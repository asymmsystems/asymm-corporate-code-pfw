Asymm Systems was born out of the open-source community. Our founder, Fahim Farook, has spent over a decade contributing to the tools that power the modern web.

### Active Contributions
Our team actively contributes to several key pillars of the Java ecosystem:
- **Spring Cloud Netflix**: Enhancing cloud-native patterns for large-scale deployments.
- **Spring Boot**: Streamlining microservices development.
- **Debezium**: Improving change data capture for modern data architectures.

### Why We Give Back
Open source is the laboratory where industry standards are tested and refined. By contributing to upstream projects, we ensure that the foundation our products build upon is stable, secure, and future-forward.

We believe that professional software is built on the shoulders of open-source giants. We are committed to maintaining that heritage.
