Asymm Systems grew out of open source. Our founder, Fahim Farook, has spent fifteen years building telecom and JVM systems, and has contributed to open-source Java projects many teams depend on.

### Where we contribute
- **Spring Cloud Netflix**
- **Spring Boot**
- **Jasypt Spring Boot**
- **Debezium**

### Why it matters to us
Our products run on the JVM and the wider Java ecosystem. Contributing to these projects keeps that foundation solid for everyone who builds on it, including us. It's also where the hard problems get worked out in the open.
