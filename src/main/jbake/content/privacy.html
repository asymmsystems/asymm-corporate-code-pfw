Asymm Systems

Privacy practices

Asymm Systems collects minimal personal data. This page documents what we collect, why, and how to control it.

Waitlist subscriber data

When you sign up for early access notifications about an Asymm product (Eliya FIPS variant, .deb / .rpm packages, macOS binary, Asymm Forensics, etc.), we collect:

  • Email address (required): to send you the notifications you signed up for
  • Use case description (optional): to help us understand who's waiting and why
  • Timestamp of signup: for operational reasons
  • Confirmation status: whether you've completed the double opt-in confirmation

We do NOT collect

  • Your name (unless you provide it in the optional use case field)
  • Your IP address (beyond what's required for spam prevention during signup)
  • Tracking cookies
  • Any analytics on your behaviour

What we DO with this data

  • Send you product-launch notifications when the products you signed up for ship
  • Send you quarterly progress updates about those products
  • That's it.

What we will NEVER do

  • Share it with third parties
  • Sell it to anyone
  • Use it for advertising
  • Use it for any product other than the ones you signed up for
  • Send you unsolicited marketing about unrelated topics

Your rights

Unsubscribe. Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe from a product list, you stop receiving emails from that list immediately.

Data export. Email privacy@asymm.systems to request an export of all personal data we hold about you. We respond within 30 days.

Data deletion. Email privacy@asymm.systems to request deletion of your personal data. We delete within 30 days and confirm by email.

GDPR. EU subscribers have full GDPR rights including data portability, rectification, and the right to object to processing. Email privacy@asymm.systems.

GitHub and public repository contributions

If you contribute to Asymm's public repositories on GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions), GitHub's privacy policies apply. Asymm does not aggregate or process this data beyond GitHub's normal operations.

Website analytics

Asymm's website (asymm.systems) does not use third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Heap, Amplitude, etc.). We do not track visitor behaviour across the site.

Server access logs are retained for 30 days for operational reasons (debugging, security incident response). They are not used for marketing or analytics.

CascadePlan for Jira Cloud

CascadePlan is an Atlassian Forge app. This section states how it handles End User Data (Atlassian Marketplace Partner Agreement, section 8.4(e)).

  • What it accesses and processes: it reads the project's Jira issues (keys, ids, summaries, types, status, assignee, estimates, due dates, parents, links) as the signed-in user, to show the plan. It stores planning data: the row order, columns and milestones of each plan, the plan list, baselines (issue id and key, Jira estimate, start, end, done) and scope-change requests. It sets two issue properties on your issues: cascadeplan.dates (start and end) and cascadeplan.links (dependency trigger percents).
  • Personal data: the Atlassian account id of the person who froze a baseline, of who requested or reviewed a scope change, and of each person who works on a task through a Jira sub-task. These account ids are reported each week to Atlassian's personal data reporting service, and the records of a closed account are changed to name no one. A contributor name that matches no account (for example a placeholder role) is kept as typed. No email addresses or other Jira text are stored.
  • Sharing: none. CascadePlan makes no calls outside your Atlassian site, and no data is sent to Asymm Systems or any third party.
  • Where it is stored: in Atlassian's Forge hosted storage for your site, which Atlassian hosts in your site's data residency location (for example the EU, the US or Australia), and in your Jira issues.
  • Responsibility: Asymm Systems, not Atlassian, is responsible for CascadePlan's processing of this data.
  • Deletion: deleting a plan deletes its data. After you uninstall the app, Atlassian keeps Forge hosted storage for 28 days and then deletes it. CascadePlan does not remove its issue properties when it is uninstalled; they stay with the issues until the issues are deleted. For other requests, write to the privacy contact below.

Privacy contact

Privacy questions, concerns, or requests: privacy@asymm.systems. Response target: within 7 days for questions, within 30 days for data requests.

Updates to this policy

When we update this privacy policy, the change is documented in the policy's git history. For substantive changes that affect how we use your data, we email subscribers before the change takes effect.
