Privacy Policy

We believe in data minimalism. This policy outlines exactly what we collect and how we use it. Honest and brief.

1. Data Collection (Survey)

When you participate in our Surveys, we collect the responses you provide. Optional identifying information (Name, Email, Organisation) is only collected if you choose to provide it. This data is used exclusively for aggregated industry research.

2. No Tracking Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies, third-party analytics (like Google Analytics), or any other mechanisms designed to track your behavior across the web.

3. Form Submissions

Data submitted via our forms is processed securely through our third-party service provider solely for the purpose of receiving and responding to your inquiry. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. PDPA & Data Requests

In accordance with the Sri Lankan Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the right to request access to or deletion of any personal data we hold. For such requests, contact privacy@asymm.systems.
