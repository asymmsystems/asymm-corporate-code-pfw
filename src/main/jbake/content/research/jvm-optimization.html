JVM Optimisation for High-Throughput BSS

Measuring the performance impact of Eliya JDK patches on modern telecom workloads and real-world BSS traffic patterns.

This study is currently in its design phase. We are developing benchmarks that simulate real-world telecom traffic to measure GC pauses and jitter across different JDK distributions.

Results will be published as a technical brief in The Registry once the fieldwork is complete.
