title=JVM Optimisation for High-Throughput BSS description=Measuring the performance impact of Eliya JDK patches on modern telecom workloads and real-world BSS traffic patterns. type=project field=JVM RUNTIME format=BENCHMARK lifecycle=UPCOMING TRACK status=published ~~~~~~ This study is currently in its design phase. We are developing benchmarks that simulate real-world telecom traffic to measure GC pauses and jitter across different JDK distributions.

"In high-throughput BSS, a 10ms jitter isn't just a lag; it's a cascade waiting to happen."

Results will be published as a technical brief in The Registry once the fieldwork is complete.
